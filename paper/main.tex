\subsection{Risk Metrics}
\paragraph{Value at Risk.} Empirical quantile of simulated terminal log-returns at
$\alpha \in \{0.95, 0.99\}$.
\paragraph{Expected Shortfall.} Conditional mean of returns below VaR$_\alpha$.
\paragraph{Tail Coverage Ratio.} Backtesting metric --- fraction of holdout returns
breaching GBM-implied VaR threshold, normalised by the expected breach rate.
